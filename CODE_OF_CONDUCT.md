# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make this project welcoming, safe, and equitable for all. We are committed to
fostering an environment that respects the dignity, rights, and contributions of everyone,
regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity,
disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language,
philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic position, level of
education, or other status.

## Encouraged behaviors

- Respect the purpose of the project and the people using it.
- Engage kindly and honestly.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Take responsibility for your actions and contributions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Repair harm when it occurs.

## Restricted behaviors

Harassment, character attacks, inciting conflict, stereotyping or discrimination,
sexualization, violating confidentiality, endangerment, divisive off-topic content,
offensive content in any publicly displayed identifier, impersonation, failing to credit
sources, advertising or spam, unsolicited mentions/DMs, and other unprofessional conduct.

## Reporting

Report concerns to the maintainer, **privately**:

- Email: the address listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile
  ([github.com/rahulshahDEV](https://github.com/rahulshahDEV)), or
- If the matter is not sensitive, open an issue.

Include what happened, when and where, who was involved, links to any public record, and
whether the situation is ongoing. Reports are handled confidentially. You will get an
acknowledgement promptly and a resolution or status update within a reasonable period.

## Enforcement

Violations are handled on a ladder proportionate to the harm:

1. **Warning** — private written warning; repair by acknowledgement/apology.
2. **Limited activity** — warning plus a time-limited cooldown from specific channels.
3. **Temporary suspension** — conditions for return; time to reflect.
4. **Permanent ban** — for repeated or severe violations where other steps failed.

Enforcement is carried out privately with the people involved. A public transparency note
may be published when it cannot harm anyone and anonymity can be preserved.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions) and when representing the project
in public. Behavior outside project spaces may be considered as supporting evidence when it
establishes a pattern or a risk to the community.
